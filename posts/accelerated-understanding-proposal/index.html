Abstract

Your careers page says official openings will be posted soon, and asks anyone already excited about physical AI to get on your radar early. This is that email, with the work attached.

I should say the disqualifying thing first. I am an engineer. I have not trained a frontier model, I have not published in physics, and I could not derive your 4D formulation if you gave me a month. What I build is the layer around the model: harnesses, gateways, orchestration, gates, the plumbing that decides whether a research idea takes a day or a fortnight to test. I am not going to dress that up as something else.

But I read your Self-Improvement section carefully, and it describes a shape I know well. Define a task, simulate, take a step in the improvement direction, repeat, with a check that a result satisfies the governing equation. That is a harness with a verifiable gate. It is the thing I have spent two years building for software delivery, where the gate is a test suite rather than a conservation law. Different domain. Same structure.

So I would like to spend a quarter on the parts of your work that are systems problems rather than science problems: the orchestration under hundreds of pre-training runs, the harness around the improvement loop, and the tooling around your scaling experiments. Three things a researcher should not be spending a Tuesday on.

Unpaid, on my own compute, with a named deliverable at each of four stages, and you can stop after any of them. I bring my own cloud credits so my learning curve does not appear on your bill. The only thing I am asking you to spend is a small amount of attention.

Why free. I want to get closer to real science than engineering has taken me so far. I keep building better tools, and I keep noticing that the interesting part is the thing the tools are pointed at. Salary is the thing I am most willing to trade for proximity to that. If a door opened from the engineering side into the research side, I would walk through it and not look back. I am telling you that at the start so you can read everything below in the right light.

Methodology

Each part below states one thing I believe about your situation, and what I would do about it. Everything I claim about Accelerated Understanding comes from your own site, your longform piece, and your public talks. Please correct anything I have wrong, because the rest of this depends on it.

1The honest boundary, before anything else

The failure mode of a letter like this is a person who overstates what they can do, gets invited in, and then costs a senior researcher three weeks of supervision. I would rather draw the line myself, in public, and be held to it.

I am not asking to be taught full time. I am asking to be useful on the left column while I read my way toward the right one, and I expect that to take years rather than months. The left column is real work that somebody at your company is doing today instead of doing physics.

2Hundreds of pre-training runs is a systems problem before it is a science problem

You have completed hundreds of pre-training runs at sizes up to a trillion parameters, with two to six petabytes moving through an average run, and scaling experiments up to 35T. At that volume the research question stops being the only bottleneck. Launch, checkpoint, resume, compare, and reproduce become the bottleneck, and every hour of that is an hour a researcher is not thinking.

This is unglamorous and it is exactly the work I am good at. Checkpoint cadence and integrity, clean resume paths, config lineage so that a run six months old can be explained, a single place to diff two runs, and alerting that fires on a stall rather than a crash. None of it requires me to understand your physics, and all of it returns hours to people who do.

3Your improvement loop is a harness, and harnesses are what I build

Your site puts it plainly. Physical laws for most areas are known, it is easy to test that a result satisfies the governing equation, and it is just hard to satisfy. Then you use that signal to drive an improvement loop without relying on data alone, and you apply the same hill-climbing to invention: define, simulate, improve, repeat.

I have written and shipped this structure repeatedly, for software rather than physics. The pattern I published as From Agents to Harnesses is five control surfaces (commands, scripts, hooks, gates, and a state machine) that pin down the phase boundaries an autonomous loop is not allowed to cross. I applied the same idea to legal work in the determinism spectrum, where the question is which parts of a workflow must never be left to chance.

You have something almost nobody I have worked with has: a gate that is objectively checkable. Most harnesses I build are gated by a judge model or a human, because nothing better exists. A conservation law is a better gate than either. That is the single most exciting sentence on your website to me, and it is why I wrote this rather than another proposal.

4Your context problem is where I would be the student, and I will not pretend otherwise

You have achieved up to a trillion context at training and beyond five trillion at inference, without patching or sub-sampling, because for physics the context scales in four dimensions rather than one. I have done long-context work, and I want to be precise about how far away from yours it is.

My work here is a gateway called Strata and a layer called Operant, which sit between an application and a model API, shape the kv-cache, compress stale context, and route repeated tasks to cheaper models once a gate says the quality holds. That is memory accounting, sharding discipline, and refusing to optimise anything before measuring it. What transfers is the discipline. What does not transfer is the hard part.

So on this axis I would not touch your architecture. I would build the instrumentation around the scaling experiments, make the profiling legible, and make it cheap to run the next one. Then I would read, and ask questions in the margins of other people's work, and expect to be useful here in year two rather than month two.

6The compute is mine, so my learning costs you nothing

The usual objection to an eager outsider is that curiosity is expensive. Someone has to pay for the runs they burn while they work out what they are doing. I would like to remove that objection entirely by paying for it myself.

So every prototype I build, every harness I test, and every mistake I make while getting up to speed lands on my bill. If I am useless, the only thing you have lost is the time it took to read this and take one call.

7Why I am offering to work for nothing, when I do have other options

I want to answer this before you wonder about it, because an offer like mine usually means somebody has run out of alternatives. I am in Antler and raising for my own company. I could spend the next year building it, and it would probably work well enough.

I would rather spend that year near this. Everything I have built so far is infrastructure for other people's intelligence, and it has been good work, and it is not the thing I actually want. What I want is to be close enough to real science to eventually do some. Your company is the clearest statement I have read of the idea that the bottleneck on discovery is now execution, and that a model which understands physics can move it. If I can make that loop run faster while I learn, that is a good trade for me even if it never becomes a job.

And to be direct about the ending I am hoping for. If the quarter goes well, I would like to keep going, on whatever terms make sense to you. If a path ever opened from the engineering side of your company into the research side, I would take it. That is the ambition. It is not a condition.

An unpaid volunteer who will not leave is worse than no volunteer. So the exit should be easier than the entry, and it should be written down before we start rather than negotiated later.

The cost of trying this is one call. The cost of ending it is one sentence.

Non-Goals

Limits

I am not claiming I can contribute to your model. Not the 4D formulation, not the super-resolution behaviour, not the multi-physics training mix, and not the scaling decisions. If I ever can, it will be because I spent years earning it, and it will not be because of anything in this document.

I am not asking for a title, a salary, equity, a decision now, or a promise about later. I am not asking anyone to mentor me on a schedule. And I am not offering compute at your scale, because I do not have it.

The claim is narrow and I would rather keep it that way. There is systems work sitting between your researchers and their next result, and I can do that work well, starting now, for free.

Next steps

  4. The end of the quarter, and an honest conversation. You decide. Keep going on some footing, or stop. If it is stop, I will have learned more in three months than in the previous year, and I will say thank you and mean it.
