Setting

Active bushfire firegrounds: heat beyond consumer drone ratings (~40°C ambient ceilings), LiPo derating and runaway risk, IMU/barometric drift in thermal gradients, smoke defeating optical navigation, ash ingress, fire-generated turbulence, GPS/comms degradation — an offline environment for hours to days.

Hardware

High-temperature composite airframe; sealed avionics bay with phase-change thermal buffering ("guaranteed minutes over the fire" — the downhole-tooling pattern); radiometric thermal + multispectral payload with edge inference for spot-fire and ember-attack detection; mesh radio with store-and-forward to a hardened ground node.

Buyer

State fire agencies (NSW RFS, CFA, QFES) and utilities under mandated bushfire inspection obligations. Procurement precedent the team knows firsthand: NSW's ~$40M shark-monitoring drone program proves agencies buy drone surveillance as a service. Insurers are the second-order data customer.

Why now

Post-Black-Summer funding; lengthening fire seasons; CASA BVLOS pathways maturing; radiometric thermal + edge-AI cost collapse. None of the four held five years ago.

Team fit

The only concept where every member is load-bearing: drone pair (platform, orchestration, vision), PhD (airframe, thermal payload, qualification), simulation (Monte Carlo fire behavior = the answer to "test what you can't replicate," alongside prescribed burns), agentic engineer (auditable deterministic autonomy), commercial (government motion), economics (service vs. crewed-aircraft cost case).

VerdictLead pitch. Highest urgency + total fit; emotionally resonant and sovereign-relevant, with a named procurement precedent.

Setting

Offshore wind farms: salt-fog corrosion destroying unsealed electronics and connectors, sustained winds beyond consumer control authority, spray ingress, long over-water transits with no recovery — and crewed visits priced in vessel day-rates and weather windows.

Offshore developers/operators, O&M contractors, and blade OEMs under warranty exposure. Blade failures rank among the costliest O&M events; today's inspection is rope access or visual-only drone imagery — surface methods blind to exactly what thermography sees.

The convergence — strategic finding

The top three are one platform wearing different clothes.

Emberhawk, offshore wind NDT, and Deepsight share a single architecture: a hardened aerial/resident sensing platform, a thermography-anchored payload, deterministic natural-language orchestration, and simulation-driven analytics. The team's best pitch is one platform company — sensing hardware for places that destroy electronics — choosing among three beachheads, with Tier 2 as expansion modules (4 extends Deepsight, 5 extends Emberhawk, 6–7 share the resident nest) and Tier 3 as breadth evidence. The business charges for retrieved intelligence, not deployed mass.

Excluded — no team fit

Rad-hard / space-grade silicon, orbital compute hardware, radiation-testing infrastructure — no semiconductor, spacecraft, or accelerator capability in the team; the orbital enthusiasm is best deployed as unit-economics rigor inside the chosen pitch. High-temperature downhole electronics for geothermal — requires SiC/Ga₂O₃ device and packaging expertise, and the field is tilting toward fiber-optic sensing with surface compute. Deep-subsea pressure vessels and underwater data centers — marine engineering the team lacks; the real subsea openings favor acoustics specialists. Antarctic/polar instrumentation — real gap, tiny procurement base, no edge over incumbent polar programs.
